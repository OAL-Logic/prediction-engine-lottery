### Use the Following Pattern to Play Powerball

#### Tips 1. Choose Numbers From Different Groups

**Powerball** has 69 numbers, Number sets can be mixed freely according to your needs. For example, we can split these numbers into 7 groups 1-10, 11-20, 21-30, 31-40, 41-50, 51-69, 61-69.

We analyzed all **Powerball** historical data, most of them spread across different groups. However, sometimes, one or more of the number groups is missing. The more groups that are split, the more groups that are missed.

#### Tips 2. Choose Low Number and High Number Ratio

Split the **70** numbers into two groups. The low half is 01 to 34, and the high half is 35 to 69. We have analyzed many lottery draw results, most winning numbers are made up of low field and high field. In rare cases, the winning numbers are all low field numbers or all high field numbers.

Basically, there are only 1-4 winning numbers in a low group or high group. **The winning numbers most occurrence ratio of low to high are 3:2, 2:3, 1: 4, 4: 1**.

#### Tips 3. Choose Odd Number and Even Number Ratio

We have analyzed many lottery draw results, most winning numbers are made up of odd numbers and even numbers. In rare cases, the winning numbers are all odd numbers or all even numbers.

Split the numbers into two groups. odd numbers in a group, and even numbers in another group. Basically, there are only 1-4 winning numbers in an odd group or even group. Counting all the all odd/even groups, there are no combinations where all 5 numbers are even and only 1 combination where all 5 numbers are odd. **The winning numbers most occurrence ratio of odd to even are 3:2, 2:3, 1: 4, 4: 1**.

#### Tips 4. Choose Adjacent Numbers of Previous Winning Numbers

What is the adjacent number? If number 21 has been hit in the last drawn, its adjacent numbers(19, 20, 22, 23) are likely to be drawn.

Analyzed all **Powerball** historical data. We found a pattern by looking at the drawings trend chart, a number that appeared in the previous drawn has a high probability of appearing in its adjacent numbers.

#### Tips 5. Use Numbers Sum Value to Exclude Some Combinations

We analyzed all **Powerball** historical data and came to the conclusion that the sums of the winning numbers are basically in a range. The sum range was basically 85-280. **So before betting on numbers, exclude combinations where the sum is too low or too high.**

#### Tips 6. Use Numbers Average Value to Exclude Some Combinations

We analyzed all **Powerball** historical data and came to the conclusion that the Average Value of the winning numbers are basically in a range. The Average Value range was basically 17-58. **So before betting on numbers, exclude combinations where the sum is too low or too high.**

#### Tips 7. Use AC Value to Exclude Some Combinations

We analyzed all **Powerball** historical data, The most often occurring [AC Value](https://example.org/lottery-studio) are 4-6, over 90%.

## Clean-Room Python Boilerplate

```python
from dataclasses import dataclass
from typing import Callable

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class RangeFilter:
    tag: int
    name: str
    metric_fn: Callable[[list[int]], int]
    min_val: int
    max_val: int

    def evaluate(self, ticket: list[int]) -> bool:
        val = self.metric_fn(ticket)
        return self.min_val <= val <= self.max_val

def evaluate_filter_suite(
    ticket: list[int],
    filters: list[RangeFilter],
    mode: str = "AND",
    n_of_m: tuple[int, int] | None = None,
) -> bool:
    passed = sum(1 for f in filters if f.evaluate(ticket))
    if mode == "AND":
        return passed == len(filters)
    if mode == "OR":
        return passed >= 1
    if n_of_m is not None:
        lo, hi = n_of_m
        return lo <= passed <= hi
    return True
```
