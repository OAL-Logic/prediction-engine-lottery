| **8** | `geomagnetic` | Space Weather. Correlates draw variance with the Solar K-Index (using local cached data) to adjust randomness temperature dynamically. | ✅ Complete |
| **9** | `cpu_entropy` | Hardware Entropy. Harvests microscopic CPU timing jitters to seed true chaotic randomness into the generation process. | ✅ Complete |
| **10** | `game_theory` | Crowd Antagonism. Explicitly models and avoids the "Birthday Bias" (1-31) and geometric center patterns to maximize EV on shared jackpots. | ✅ Complete |
