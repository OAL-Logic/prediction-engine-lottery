# Absurdity Engine: Legacy v2.0 Roadmap

This document outlines the early theoretical framework and sprint tasks for the "Absurdity Engine" v2.0. It is preserved here for historical reference.

## 1. The Statistical Elasticity Model (Hard Math)
**Core Concept:** Information Entropy & Mean Reversion.
- **Z-Score Deviation:** Instead of tracking "hot" numbers, the engine tracks the "Elasticity" of a number—how far it has strayed from its expected probability mean. This treats draw history as a spring that must eventually snap back to equilibrium.
- **Adversarial Crowd Modeling:** To maximize **Expected Value (EV)**, the algorithm identifies "socially unpopular" numbers. By avoiding common human biases (birthdays 1-31, arithmetic sequences, round numbers), the engine ensures that a jackpot win is less likely to be shared.
- **Combinatorial Steiner Wheels:** Moves from single-ticket prediction to "Pool Coverage." By using Steiner Triple Systems, the engine generates the minimal set of tickets required to guarantee a sub-prize if N numbers from a larger pool are hit.

## 2. The Atmospheric Bias Engine (Environmental Chaos)
**Core Concept:** Physical Entrainment.
- **Fluid Dynamics:** Most lottery machines are pneumatic. Variations in **Barometric Pressure, Humidity, and Temperature** affect air density in the blower, which in turn alters the drag coefficients of the balls.
- **Ball Weight Variance:** Even standardized lottery balls have microscopic weight differences due to paint thickness or rubber wear.
- **Implementation:** Correlate historical draws with Open-Meteo archive data. A "Neural Density Map" identifies which numbers are physically favored under specific atmospheric "Density Buckets" (e.g., High Humidity > 80%).

## 3. The Sacred Manifold (Esoteric/Quantum)
**Core Concept:** Personal Vibrational Resonance.
- **Non-Euclidean Mapping:** The 60-number grid is treated as a **3D Torus** or Dodecahedron. Past hits are mapped as vertices, and the next numbers are predicted based on **Geometric Harmonics** and interference patterns.
- **Kabbalistic Arcano Triggers:** Integrates the "Inverted Triangle of Life" (from `kabbalistic.py`) with temporal cycles.
    - **Arcano 78 (Sudden Windfall):** Triggers a "High-Risk" mode (increased temperature) in the sampling logic.
    - **Arcano 16 (The Tower):** Triggers a "Caution" mode, recommending a skip or a low-investment strategy.
- **Quantum Biorhythms:** Uses the user's name as a seed for a pseudo-quantum random number generator, which is then filtered through a "Sacred Geometry" manifold (The Flower of Life) to find the intersection between user identity and numerical probability.
