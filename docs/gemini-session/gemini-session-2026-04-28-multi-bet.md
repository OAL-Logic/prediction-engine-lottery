## 🛠️ Technical Details

### Combinatorial Odds
For a lottery where you drawn $k$ numbers from $n$, and you play $m$ numbers ($m > k$), the number of ways to win the jackpot is $C(m, k)$. Your odds improve from $1$ in $C(n, k)$ to $C(m, k)$ in $C(n, k)$, which is equivalent to $1$ in $\frac{C(n, k)}{C(m, k)}$.

### Efficiency Parity
The "Efficiency Score" ($\frac{\log_{10}(odds)}{price}$) typically remains stable or slightly improves for multiple bets, but the absolute capital requirement increases exponentially.
