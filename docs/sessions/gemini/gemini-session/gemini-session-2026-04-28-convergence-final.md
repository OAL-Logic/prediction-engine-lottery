### Linear Algebra & Statistics
The Z-Score calculation uses the analytical standard deviation derived from the binomial distribution expectation: $\sigma = \sqrt{n \cdot p \cdot (1-p)}$. This ensures the "hotness" metric is objective and mathematically sound for any pool size.
