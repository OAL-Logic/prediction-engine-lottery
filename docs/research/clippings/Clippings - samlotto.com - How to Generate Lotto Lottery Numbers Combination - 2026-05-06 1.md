If you play the 3-digit 4-digit lottery visit: How to Generate [Pick 3 Pick 4 Lottery Combinations](https://www.samlotto.com/how-to-generate-pick-3-pick-4-lottery-combinations/).

Next I will describe in detail these 3 ways of generating combinations.

## Generating Combinations Standard

You can quickly generate all possible combinations(Full Generator), and [abbreviated wheeling(Wheels Generator)](https://www.samlotto.com/how-to-use-the-wheeling-systems-to-generate-combinations/) combinations, If the ticket contains Bonus numbers, you can also choose to generate a combination with Bonus numbers.

#### Quick Selection of Numbers

In the Pick panel, by clicking on the “ **Pick** ” button, you can quickly generate a pool of numbers from the results of the previous draws. We offer 3 modes of picking numbers picking hot numbers, picking cold numbers, and picking previous numbers.

#### Generate all Possible Combinations

If your numbers pool contains the jackpot, to win the jackpot, you must use the full generator to [generate all possible](https://www.samlotto.com/how-to-generate-all-possible-combinations-of-lottery-tickets/) [combinations](https://www.samlotto.com/how-to-generate-all-possible-combinations-of-lottery-tickets/).

#### Generate Wheeling Combinations

**SamLotto lottery software** support **[use the wheeling systems to generate tickets](https://www.samlotto.com/how-to-use-the-wheeling-systems-to-generate-combinations/),** This abbreviated wheeling system contains over 3800 wheeling formulas, support almost all pick 2, pick 3, pick 4, pick 5, pick 6, pick 7 from 1 to 90 numbers, and Supports selection of up to 36 numbers. The wheeling system doesn’t guarantee you’ll win the big prize, but it does guarantee you’ll win lots of little prizes, and if you’re lucky enough, you’ll win the big prize! For example: If you see “if 6 match 5,” you’ll need five winning numbers to be among those you picked in the wheel. If that requirement is met, you’ll have at least one winning ticket in the tier that requires guessing four numbers right.

#### Generate Combinations Containing Bonus Number

We know that in some of the most popular lotteries around the world, there is one bonus number, two bonusnumbers, or even three bonus numbers, where all the numbers and bonus numbers are guessed to win the prize pool. In the Powerball lottery the Bonus is called red Powerball, In the Mega Millions, the bonus called gold Mega Ball. **SamLotto** supports [adding Bonus ticket combinations](https://www.samlotto.com/generate-lotterycombination-containing-bonus-number/).
