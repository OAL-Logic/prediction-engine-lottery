Inserting a Draw		   

At times it may be necessary to insert a drawing that was previously entered in a drawing file.

　

To insert a drawing:

1.On the Toolbar, click the Drawings button open the edit drawings window.

2. Click on the drawing that you want to insert in the drawing list.

3. Click the Insert button open the Insert Drawings window:



Selecting a date and enter your numbers in the edit box then click the Ok button.

4. Click on the Yes button when you are asked to confirm.
