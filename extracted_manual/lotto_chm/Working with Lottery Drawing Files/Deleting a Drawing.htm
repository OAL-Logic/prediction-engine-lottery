Deleting a Draw		   


At times it may be necessary to delete a drawing that was previously entered in a drawing file.



To Delete a drawing:

1.On the Toolbar, click the Drawings button open the edit drawings form.

2. Click on the drawing that you want to delete in the drawing list.

3. Click on the Delete button.

4. Click the Yes button when you are asked to confirm the deletion.
