Modifying a Draw		   

At times it may be necessary to modify a drawing that was previously entered in the drawing file.

To modify a drawing:

1.On the Toolbar, click the Drawings button open edit drawings window.

2. Click on the drawing that you want to modify in the drawing list. The date and numbers are displayed in the edit box.

3. Editing edit box.

4. Click the Modify button to save your changes.
