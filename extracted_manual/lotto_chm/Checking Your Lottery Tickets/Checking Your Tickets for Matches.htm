Check Winning		  

Before you check winning, you should ensure that your drawing file is the latest. SamLotto will check the lottery tickets that in Tickets Stroe panel.

In the toolbar, click " Check Win" button open the check winning windo.



How to checking win tickets?

鈶? Choose a draw.

鈶? Choose matching =, >, <, >= or <=.

鈶? Choose matching amount of numbers.

鈶? Click "Check Now" button.

The correct tickets will be displayed in the matching tickets panel.

Check Bonus numbers

If the current lottery supports bonus numbers, the Bonus checkbox will display. If checked, only combinations that match the bonus number will also be displayed.

To Save Matching Tickets:

Right-click pop-up menu



Save To Html - Save the correct tickets to htm file.
Save To Txt - Save the correct tickets to txt file.
Save To Excel - Save the correct tickets to xls file.
Clear - Clear the correct tickets.
