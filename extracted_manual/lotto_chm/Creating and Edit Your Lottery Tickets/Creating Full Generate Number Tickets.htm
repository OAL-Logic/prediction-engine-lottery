Creating Full Generate Number Tickets		  

To Creating Full Generate Number Tickets:

Setup 1: In the numbers panel, selecting your like numbers, If the number is selected will become Red. If you want to cancel selection, please use the mouse to click the specified number.

Setup 2: In the radio box, selected Full Generator.

Setup 3: Click Generate Tickets button.



Click OK, the all tickes will be saved in the tickets panel.
