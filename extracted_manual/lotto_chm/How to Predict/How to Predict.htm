How to Predict		   

1. To predict lottery: On the Toolbar, click the Prediction button then click Predictor open the predictor tab. Select a formula click Predict Now button to predict next drawing numbers now.






2. To add the prediction numbers to picking numbers panel: click the button pop-up dialog box, and then click the Yes button.
