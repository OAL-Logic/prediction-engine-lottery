How Wheeling System works		   

It's incredibly simple to put a Wheeling System to work for you! Let's walk though an example.
On this sample wheel, we're wheeling eight different numbers for a pick 6 lotto game, generating 28 different combinations. Our eight numbers are 05, 09, 16, 23, 26, 31, 37 and 45. Let's place each number under a letter - A through H - in ascending order, as you can see below.

A B C D E F G H
05 09 16 23 26 31 37 45

If all 6 official winning numbers are among the 8 numbers wheeled, you are guaranteed to have
  • 1 ticket to win the jackpot
  • 11 tickets that match 5 of 6
  • 16 tickets that match 4 of 6

  • 2 tickets that match 5 of 6
  • 15 tickets that match 4 of 6
  • 11 tickets that match 3 of 6

  • 5 tickets that match 4 of 6
